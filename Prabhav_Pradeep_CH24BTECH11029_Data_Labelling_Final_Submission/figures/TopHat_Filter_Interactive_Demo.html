Top-hat: push a flat coin up from below

Brightness along one line through a cell. Wherever the coin fits, the labeller calls it "background" and subtracts it.

1. The image, and the "ground" the coin traces

image brightness ground traced by the coin the coin, pushed up as far as it fits

2. What the labeller sees: image − ground

true granule (height above the glow) top-hat result

The coin is the top-hat's disk seen from the side: a disk of radius r lying flat is a bar 2r+1 px wide. A granule narrower than the coin is kept whole; a granule wider than the coin lets the coin rise inside it, so most of it is subtracted as background. Our largest granules are about 44 px across, so the coin must be wider than that: r = 30 gives 61 px.
